What SgSL is made of

The routes on the globe left layers in one language. SgSL is what NTU calls an amalgamation of Shanghainese Sign Language, American Sign Language, manually coded English and locally derived signs. Choose a layer to see its representative terms.

This diagram shows order of arrival, not size. No study has measured what share of SgSL comes from each source, so any pie chart of SgSL's origins would be made up.

What every sign is built from

Whatever its source, a sign in SgSL combines five parameters. Change one and you can get a different sign.

Handshape
1, 3, 5, A, B, Baby O, Flat-O
Orientation
Palm in, out, up, down, sideways
Location
Forehead, chin, chest, neutral space
Movement
Path, wrist, fingers
Non-manual
Face, mouthing, head, body
